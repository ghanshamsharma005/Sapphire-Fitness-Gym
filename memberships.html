Frequently Asked Questions

Are the prices on this website final?

No. The figures shown on this prototype (₹999, ₹2,499, etc.) are demonstration estimates for this website project. The gym management will provide official fee structures upon in-person or WhatsApp enquiry.

What documents are required for admission?

A government-issued photo ID (such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, or driving license), contact phone number, and emergency contact details are typically required to initiate membership.

Do you provide trial workout sessions?

Yes! You can schedule a gym floor tour and discussion by submitting the enquiry form or sending a message on WhatsApp.

Are changing rooms and lockers available?

Yes. As shown in our authentic photographs, Sapphire Fitness Gym provides private full-height changing cubicles and numbered 12-door individual key-locked lockers for members during their training sessions.
